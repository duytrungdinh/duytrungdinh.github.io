TerraAgentbot

A personal AI assistant for one user.

TerraAgentbot is a private, self-hosted assistant run by its owner, Dinh Duy Trung. It connects to the owner's own Google account so that it can read and update the owner's files in Google Drive, Google Sheets and Google Docs, for example to keep a personal trading journal up to date.

The app is not offered to the public. Only the owner signs in to it, and it does not access any other person's Google account.

Read how the app handles Google user data in the Privacy Policy.
