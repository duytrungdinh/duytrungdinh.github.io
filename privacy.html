Privacy Policy

TerraAgentbot · Last updated: 9 November 2026

TerraAgentbot is a personal assistant used only by its owner, Dinh Duy Trung. This policy explains what Google user data the app accesses and how that data is used.

Data the app accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app may access the Google Drive, Google Sheets and Google Docs files that the owner grants it permission to use, along with basic account information (name and email address) needed to sign in.

How the data is used

The data is used only to perform tasks the owner asks for, such as reading, creating and updating the owner's own documents and spreadsheets. The app does not use Google user data for advertising, does not sell it, and does not share it with any third party.

Storage and security

Sign-in tokens are stored on the owner's own computer or server. The app does not keep copies of file contents beyond what is needed to complete a requested task.

Limited Use

TerraAgentbot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops the app from reading or changing any Google data.
